*approx. one-way phone → laptop delay, estimated from round trips.

  1. Scan the QR code with your phone camera (same Wi-Fi).
  2. Tap Enable motion sensors, hold the phone comfortably and tap Calibrate.
  3. Tilt left/right to steer. Shake to jump (and to start / restart).
  4. Orange barriers: jump over them. Red/grey: steer around.

Keyboard fallback: ← → / A D steer, Space jump/start, R restart.
